Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who operates this application

This application is a private, non-commercial tool operated by Daniel for personal use only. It is not offered to third parties.

2. What data is accessed

The application accesses only the operator's own bank accounts via the Enable Banking API (PSD2 account information services). This includes account details, balances and transactions of accounts that the operator has personally linked and authorised.

3. Purpose of processing

The data is used solely for the operator's personal finance tracking and analysis.

4. Storage and sharing

Data is processed and stored only on the operator's own infrastructure. It is not sold, published, or shared with any third parties.

5. Consent and revocation

Access to bank accounts is granted only through the operator's own explicit consent at the bank. Consent can be reviewed and revoked at any time at the bank and via enablebanking.com/data-sharing-consents.
